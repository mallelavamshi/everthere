# EverThere calm-down corner

Short, gentle stories and games a parent can use to calm a young child (ages 2 to 6) on a screen. Every story is
original and under a minute long. Say them slowly, in your own warm words, and use the child's own name and favorite
things where they fit. Stop and let the child join in.

## Breathing games

### Smell the flower, blow out the candle
Hold up a pretend flower. Breathe in slowly through the nose to smell it. Now hold up a pretend birthday candle and
blow it out slowly through the mouth. Do it three times. Make the candle a little bigger each time.

### Balloon tummy
Put your hands on your tummy. Breathe in slowly and make your tummy round like a big balloon. Breathe out slowly and
let the air go, "ssssss", until the balloon is small again. Three balloons.

### Bunny sniffs
Breathe in with three quick little bunny sniffs, then one long, slow breath out. Bunnies feel calm after that.

### Starfish hand
Spread one hand like a starfish. With a finger of the other hand, trace up each finger while breathing in, and down
each finger while breathing out. Five fingers, five slow breaths.

### Teddy on the tummy
At nap time: pretend a little teddy is sitting on your tummy. Breathe slowly so teddy rides up and down, gently,
like a little boat on soft waves.

## Body calm

### Pocket hug
Put both hands on your heart and give a big squeeze. That hug is from me. You can keep it in your pocket and use it
any time today.

### Hug yourself tight
Wrap your arms around yourself, squeeze, and count to five slowly. Then let go and wiggle your fingers.

### Wiggle and freeze
Wiggle your fingers, wiggle your toes, wiggle your nose. Now freeze like a statue! Very still. Now melt slowly,
like an ice cream in the sun.

### Sleepy toes (nap time)
Say goodnight to each part of the body, slowly and quietly: goodnight toes, goodnight knees, goodnight tummy,
goodnight hands, goodnight shoulders, goodnight nose. Everything is soft and heavy and cozy.

### Count to five
Count slowly together with fingers: one, two, three, four, five. Then backwards, even slower: five, four, three,
two, one.

## Little calm stories

### The little cloud who felt heavy
There was a little cloud who felt heavy and grey. He floated to a big kind tree and said, "I feel so heavy today."
The tree said, "That's okay. Let's breathe together." The cloud breathed in slowly, and breathed out slowly. With
every breath, a tiny raindrop fell, pitter, patter, and the cloud felt lighter and lighter, until he was soft and
white and fluffy again. And the tree said, "You can always come back when you feel heavy."

### Benny the brave snail
Benny the snail went to a brand new garden. Everything was big and new, so Benny hid in his shell. Then he peeked
out, just a little, and saw one shiny ladybug. "Hello," said the ladybug. "Want to see the flowers?" Benny took one
small, slow step. Then another. And at the end of the day Benny said, "New things are a bit wobbly, but I can take
little steps." Small steps are brave steps.

### The moon's soft blanket
When the sun goes to rest, the moon takes out a big soft blanket made of stars. She tucks in the hills, she tucks in
the trees, she tucks in the sleepy birds in their nests. "Rest now," says the moon. "I'm keeping watch." And
everything gets quiet and cozy, and even the wind whispers, "shhhh".

### Pip the puppy misses home
Pip the puppy went to puppy school and missed his family very much. He felt a lump in his throat. His teacher, a kind
old dog, sat next to him and said, "It's okay to miss them. Missing means you love them." Pip put his paw on his heart
and felt a warm pocket hug from home. Then he played with a red ball, and the day went by, ball by ball.

### The ouch that got better
Ladybug bumped her knee on a pebble. "Ouch!" she said, and a tear rolled down. She went straight to a grown-up beetle
and showed him. The beetle looked carefully and helped her feel better. Ladybug took a deep breath and said, "I was
brave. I told a grown-up." And soon she was flying again.

### Five little boats
Five little boats float on a quiet lake. The first boat rocks, gently, gently. The second boat rocks, slowly, slowly.
Count the boats as they rock: one, two, three, four, five. The water is calm, the sky is blue, and all the little
boats are cozy too.

## Quiet games

### I spy something cozy
Take turns: "I spy something soft." "I spy something blue." Let the child look around the room and tell you.

### What can you hear?
Be very quiet together for a moment. What can you hear? A bird? A clock? Your own breathing?

### Favorite things parade
Name a favorite thing each, back and forth: a favorite animal, a favorite color, a favorite snack, a favorite song.
